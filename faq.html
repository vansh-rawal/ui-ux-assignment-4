Frequently Asked Questions

Who can participate?

Students from different colleges can participate in the fest.

Is registration required?

Yes, participants must register before participating in an event.

Is there any registration fee?

No, registration for the events is free.

Where will the events take place?

Events will be conducted at different locations inside the college campus.

Can I participate in more than one event?

Yes, students can participate in multiple events if their schedules do not conflict.
